## Recording copy

Lost far from Earth, we became refugees among the stars. To survive, we needed more than shelter.

We found Numens. Living frames of armor and circuitry, shaped by their pilots—and sometimes, by something divine.

Our journey takes us across a galaxy of dangerous missions and knowledge worth recovering.

Choose four Numens. Build your squad for ground, water, or space. Each environment demands a different team.

Then step inside—and take your squad into the unknown.

In turn-based tactical battles, use cover, coordinate abilities, and make every move count.

Recover Neural Artefacts. A discovery in space could unlock a new advantage on the ground.

Each holds a vast neural archive: knowledge that can reshape your Numen.

Choose an artefact. Link it to your frame.

Unlock new abilities, new equipment—even a second form. This is evolution you can see.

Expand your carrier: a home for your pilots, and a place to prepare for what comes next.

Their stories matter. Unlock collectible manga chapters and interactive novels. Follow their journey from displacement to rebellion—and discover what they stand for.

Corporations, alien societies, unknown life. No one believes they are the villain. Everyone seeks knowledge. Everyone has something to lose.

Your pilots. Your Numens. Your next horizon.

## Voice direction

Pronunciation: Numen = NOO-men; Numens = NOO-menz. Neural = NYOOR-uhl. Artefact = AR-tuh-fakt.

Original mature low-register narrator, natural and intimate, clear diction, restrained cinematic weight. Avoid parody, gravelly strain, constant shouting, and imitation of any real performer. Approximately 140–150 spoken words per minute in active passages with pauses around reveals. Record clean dry mono WAV, preferably 48 kHz / 24-bit, without baked-in music or reverb. Deliver each numbered shot's line separately with a little silence before and after, plus one continuous read. Record an intimate take and a slightly more energized take of the transformation and ending.

## Updated game description

Project Phoenix is a story-led squad RPG about pilots and evolving Numens, armored machines with continuously rebuilding synthetic frames. Some are guided by pilots; others may also respond to a divine presence. Players assemble squads of four for turn-based tactical missions across ground, water, and space, adapting unit selection to the environment. Cross-environment artefact rewards and story objectives encourage exploration beyond a single specialized squad.

Neural Artefacts are vast structured datasets embodied as neural-network objects. Players attach them to Numens through a simple drag-and-drop interface, unlocking abilities, equipment and secondary transformation modes. Artefacts may also be dismantled into Shards, the proposed hard currency.

The galaxy-spanning story follows displacement from Earth, refuge and rebellion. Corporations, human communities, alien societies and unknown life pursue survival and knowledge through competing interests and fears. Pilots' personal stories can be unlocked and collected as manga chapters and interactive visual novels. The carrier expands alongside the squad as its mobile home and preparation base.
